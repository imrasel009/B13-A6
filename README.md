**Project Name**
Digital Tools Buying Website

**Description**
This is a website where you can buy the subscription for different tools. You can get the pro access of different AI tools that are gonna make your work more efficient and effortless.

**Technology Used**
-React.js
-Tailwind CSS, DaisyUI
-JavaScript (ES6+)
-React-Toastify (NPM Package)
-React Icons
-JSON (for product data)

**Key Features**
*Different product option in a single section.
*You can buy multiple products at once.
*You will have the cart functionality where you can see your selected product and you can remove also.
*The cart will show your selected product and the total cost.
*You can make the purchase in one click. By clicking on the Proceed to payment button.
